CHILD SAFETY STANDARDS

Vibe Chat by VIBE-CHECK07 · effective September 25, 2026

Vibe Chat has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any sexualisation of minors.

OUR STANDARDS

  • The app is for adults 18+. Accounts we believe belong to minors are removed.
  • Any CSAE content or behaviour is removed and the account permanently suspended.
  • Confirmed CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant authorities in the user's region, as required by law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal process.

HOW TO REPORT

  • In the app: tap the handle or hold the message → REPORT → Someone under 18 or Sexual or nude content. These reports are reviewed first.
  • By email: adminapps4757@gmail.com.
