PRIVACY POLICY

Vibe Chat · effective September 25, 2026

Vibe Chat ("the app") is published by VIBE-CHECK07 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept and how to delete it. Questions: adminapps4757@gmail.com.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID or browsing history. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see them.

Some settings (the message-decay timer, scanline effect, joined-room list, which photos you have unblurred) are stored only on your device.

CAMERA AND PHOTOS

The camera is used only when you tap to take a photo to post. On Android, choosing an existing photo uses the system photo picker, so the app never gets access to your photo library as a whole.

WHO CAN SEE WHAT

  • Open rooms: anyone signed in can read lines and photos posted there. Photos arrive blurred until the viewer chooses to reveal them.
  • Private channels: text messages are visible only to the two members; photos sent there can also be viewed by moderators (see below). Private channels are not end-to-end encrypted: they are protected in transit (HTTPS) and by access rules on our database.
  • Moderators: can see all open-room content and all photos, including photos sent in private channels, to keep the app safe. Private-channel text messages are seen by moderators only when someone reports them.

SERVICE PROVIDERS

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own use. We use these processors to run the app:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in, file storage and realtime delivery (supabase.com/privacy).
  • Google Play — payments and purchase verification (policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Our email provider, to send sign-up confirmations and password-reset codes.

All data is encrypted in transit.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

  • Open-room lines fade from the app after a few minutes and are deleted from our servers within 24 hours, unless they were reported.
  • Private-channel messages are kept until both members drop the channel, or until the sender deletes their account.
  • Photos, your profile and your account are kept until you delete your account.
  • Reports (including the copy of reported content) are kept for up to 12 months for safety and legal reasons.
  • Purchase records are kept, without your account link, as long as required for tax and accounting.

DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

In the app: SYS → DELETE ACCOUNT. This immediately deletes your account, handle, messages, private channels and photos. You can also request deletion without the app: see Delete your account.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used, by emailing adminapps4757@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data-protection authority. We respond within 30 days.

AGE

Vibe Chat is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor is using the app, tell us and we will remove the account.

CHANGES

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
